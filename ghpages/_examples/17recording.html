Modern audio recording using AudioWorklet for low-latency capture and real-time waveform visualization.

Features:
  • AudioWorklet-based recording (low latency, raw PCM access)
  • Real-time waveform visualization during recording
  • Microphone device selection
  • Record multiple tracks and play them back
Note: This example requires HTTPS or localhost. Make sure to grant microphone permissions when prompted.

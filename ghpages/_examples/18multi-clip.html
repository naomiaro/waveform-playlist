This demo showcases multiple audio clips per track with gaps and different timing. All source files are 30 seconds long and musically aligned. Each track contains multiple clips extracted from different portions of the source audio, demonstrating how clips can be arranged with gaps to create interesting musical arrangements.
Track & Clip Arrangement:
  • Vocals Track: Two clips (0-10s, 20-30s from source) - 10s gap cutting out the middle
  • Guitar Track: Full 30 seconds continuous playback
  • Piano Track: Two clips starting at 5s and 20s on timeline
  • Bass Track: Three clips (0-8s, 10-16s, 20-30s) with 2s and 4s gaps
  • Press Play to hear how the clips are arranged on each track!

This demo showcases multiple audio clips per track with gaps and different timing. All source files are 30 seconds long and musically aligned. Each track contains multiple clips extracted from different portions of the source audio, demonstrating how clips can be arranged with gaps to create interesting musical arrangements.
Track & Clip Arrangement:
  • Vocals Track: Two clips (source 0-10s, 20-30s) at matching timeline positions - 10s gap in the middle
  • Guitar Track: Full 30 seconds continuous playback
  • Piano Track: Two clips (source 5-15s, 20-30s) playing at matching timeline positions - musically aligned
  • Bass Track: Three clips (source 0-8s, 10-16s, 20-30s) at matching timeline positions with 2s and 4s gaps
  • Press Play to hear how the clips are arranged on each track!
Interactive Features:
  • Drag Clip Headers: Click and drag the header of any clip to move it along the timeline
  • Trim Clips: Drag the left or right edge of a clip to adjust its start/end points
  • Split Clips: Press S while playing to split the clip at the playhead position
  • Collision Detection: Clips cannot overlap - they snap to adjacent boundaries

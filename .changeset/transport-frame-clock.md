`Clock` keeps its position in integer AudioContext frames on the 128-frame render-quantum lattice; elapsed time is counted in whole frames and a `seekTo(seconds)` target round-trips bit-exactly through `getTime()`. New: `Clock.getFrame()` / `getFrameExact()` / `seekToFrame()` / `secondsToFrame()` / `sampleRate` / `rebaseCount` / `takeDiscontinuity()`, `Transport.getCurrentFrame()`, the `ContextFrame` brand (distinct from `Sample`, which is the SampleTimeline's rate), the `discontinuity` transport event, and the exported `RENDER_QUANTUM_FRAMES`. A context reading that goes backwards while playing (defensive — the spec makes `currentTime` monotonic) is absorbed by moving the anchor so the position holds, and the transport re-creates every scheduled source from the held position; a stalled reading simply freezes the position. `toAudioTime()` while running is anchor-based and never re-reads `currentTime`; stopped, it uses the same snapped reading. The Clock now rejects an AudioContext without a finite positive `sampleRate` (a `{ currentTime }`-only mock) and non-finite seeks. Existing seconds-based APIs keep their signatures.

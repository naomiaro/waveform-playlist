'@waveform-playlist/browser': major
'@waveform-playlist/recording': major

browser: `WaveformPlaylistProvider` takes a required `arrangement` prop (an `Arrangement` from `@waveform-playlist/engine`, made with `useCreateArrangement`) and no longer takes `tracks`, `onTracksChange` or `deferEngineRebuild` (#812). The arrangement owns the tracks, the mix, the undo history and the loading of the audio the clips name; the provider derives everything it publishes from it and writes nothing back. Change tracks through the arrangement's methods (`addTrack`, `removeTrack`, `updateTrack`, `setTracks`, …); below the provider the new `useArrangement()` returns it. A new `arrangement` object mounts everything below the provider again (a new engine; playback stops; zoom, selection, loop, master volume and scroll start again) and warns once in development. In plain JavaScript a leftover `tracks`, `onTracksChange` or `deferEngineRebuild` prop is ignored, and a provider without `arrangement` throws while it renders.

browser: an engine is built when the provider mounts, when `effects` changes identity and when `zoomLevels` changes content. Adding, removing or replacing tracks never builds a new engine. `onReady` and `onError` are read when they are called, so an inline function no longer rebuilds the engine. `createAdapter` and `sampleRate` are read when an engine is built. Audio is decoded with the current adapter's `AudioContext`, and nothing is fetched before the first adapter exists.

browser: removed from `@waveform-playlist/browser/tone`: `useAudioTracks`, `useDynamicTracks` and the types `AudioTrackConfig`, `TrackSource`, `TrackLoadError`, `UseDynamicTracksReturn`. A track names its audio with `audioTrack({ src | audioBuffer | waveformData, name, start, duration, offset, … })` from `@waveform-playlist/core` (`start`, not `startTime`); a dropped file is `arrangement.addTrack(audioTrack({ src: file }))`; loading state and failures are in `useArrangementSources(arrangement)`, and `arrangement.retry(src)` starts a failed source again.

browser: `usePlaylistTimeline()` no longer has `onTracksChange` (a leftover read is `undefined`). Its `tracks` is `readonly ClipTrack[]` and holds loaded clips only; `peaksDataArray` also holds a placeholder record (`isPlaceholder: true`) for each clip still loading that has a `duration` or `waveformData`, drawn dimmed without handles once the provider knows its real sample rate; such a record has no clip in `tracks`, so look records up by `clipId`. `duration` counts loaded audio only. An export started while audio loads renders the loaded clips.

browser: `usePlaylistMixer().trackStates` has an entry for every track from the moment it enters the arrangement (no `undefined` entries); the values are the arrangement's mix. `useTrack()` no longer has `isLoading`: the track controls are enabled while a track's audio loads and a change made then is kept. `TrackContextValue` has a new required field, `isRecordingArmed`.

browser: `onReady` and the window event `waveform-playlist:ready` happen once per provider mount, the first time an engine is attached and no source of the arrangement is pending (a failed source counts as settled); they are not repeated after a later engine build. The event's `detail` is `{ trackCount, duration }` (every track; the loaded audio). After the first ready, `isReady` is false only while a later engine is built. Before the first ready `play()` does nothing and queues nothing (one console warning per mount in development); edits, the mixer, `seekTo` and the selection work.

browser: a track the arrangement refuses (wrong kind, a clip still loading on a MIDI track, a duplicate track or clip id) throws at the call that offered it: a render error from the `useCreateArrangement` initializer, an exception from `arrangement.addTrack()`. `onError` is called when the engine or its adapter cannot be built (only for a build that is still current) and when `onReady` throws; a failed source does not call it.

browser: `useClipDragHandlers({ samplesPerPixel, snapSamplePosition? })` and `useClipSplitting({ samplesPerPixel })` lose the options `tracks` and `engineRef`: they read the provider's arrangement and throw outside `WaveformPlaylistProvider`. A move, trim or split made while no engine is attached is kept and played by the next engine (it was dropped with a warning); a move finds its track by the id it had at drag start. The mixer setters, `reorderTrack`, `undo` and `redo` of `useEditorControls()` work with no engine attached; an undo never changes the mix, and the history survives an engine rebuild.

browser: while a take records, the armed track is muted in the arrangement's mix: the mixer and its Mute button show it muted, its built-in `<TrackMuteButton />` is disabled, and the mute from before the take is written back when the take ends, when the armed track changes and when the provider unmounts, whatever was set in between. An engine build that fails during playback now shows playback stopped.

recording: `useIntegratedRecording(arrangement, selectedTrackId, options?)` (was `(tracks, setTracks, selectedTrackId, options?)`). The hook reads the arrangement when a take starts and ends and adds the take, with the clips it trims, removes or splits, in one `arrangement.updateTrack()`: one undo step. `startRecording()` also refuses, through `error`, a selected id the arrangement does not hold (the take used to start and be lost at the end) and a track that holds a clip whose audio is still loading, failed to load or cannot be made from its audio. A take the arrangement refuses at the end sets `error` to `Recording completed but the take could not be saved on track "<id>": …`. The package now depends on `@waveform-playlist/engine` (for the `Arrangement` type).

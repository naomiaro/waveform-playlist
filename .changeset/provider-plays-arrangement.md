browser: `WaveformPlaylistProvider` takes a required `arrangement` prop (an `Arrangement` from `@waveform-playlist/engine`, made with `useCreateArrangement`) and no longer takes `tracks`, `onTracksChange` or `deferEngineRebuild` (#812). The arrangement owns the tracks, the mix, the undo history and the loading of the audio the clips name; the provider derives everything it publishes from it and writes nothing back. Change tracks through the arrangement's methods (`addTrack`, `removeTrack`, `updateTrack`, `setTracks`, …); below the provider the new `useArrangement()` returns it. A new `arrangement` object mounts everything below the provider again (a new engine; playback stops; zoom, selection, loop, master volume and scroll start again) and warns once in development. In plain JavaScript a leftover `tracks`, `onTracksChange` or `deferEngineRebuild` prop is ignored. A provider given no `arrangement` (or anything that is not an object) throws while it renders, in every build: `` [waveform-playlist] WaveformPlaylistProvider needs an `arrangement` prop: an arrangement made with `useCreateArrangement`, which owns the tracks. The `tracks` and `onTracksChange` props were removed. See https://naomiaro.github.io/waveform-playlist/docs/react/upgrading ``.

browser: an engine is built when the provider mounts, when `effects` changes identity and when `zoomLevels` changes content. Adding, removing or replacing tracks never builds a new engine. `onReady` and `onError` are read when they are called, so an inline function no longer rebuilds the engine. `createAdapter` and `sampleRate` are read when an engine is built. Audio is decoded with the current adapter's `AudioContext`, and nothing is fetched before the first adapter exists.

browser: removed from `@waveform-playlist/browser/tone`: `useAudioTracks`, `useDynamicTracks` and the types `AudioTrackConfig`, `TrackSource`, `TrackLoadError`, `UseDynamicTracksReturn`. A track names its audio with `audioTrack({ src | audioBuffer | waveformData, name, start, duration, offset, … })` from `@waveform-playlist/core` (`start`, not `startTime`); a dropped file is `arrangement.addTrack(audioTrack({ src: file }))`; loading state and failures are in `useArrangementSources(arrangement)`, and `arrangement.retry(src)` starts a failed source again. A dropped file's track no longer starts as `clips: []` with a name ending in " (loading...)": it has its plain name and one clip still loading, which becomes a loaded clip of the same id. The load error messages changed: `Failed to fetch <label>: <status> <statusText>`, `Failed to fetch <label>` (network or cross-origin failure), `Decoded audio is empty: <label>`, the browser's own decode error; `<label>` is the URL without its query string, fragment and credentials (was `Failed to fetch <src>: <statusText>` and `Invalid audio buffer for <src>`). `useCreateArrangement` reads only `loadSource` and `undoLimit` from its options.

browser: `usePlaylistTimeline()` no longer has `onTracksChange` (a leftover read is `undefined`). Its `tracks` is `readonly ClipTrack[]` and holds loaded clips only; `peaksDataArray` also holds a placeholder record (`isPlaceholder: true`) for each clip still loading that has a `duration` or `waveformData`, drawn dimmed without handles once the provider knows its real sample rate (one with only a `duration` as a box of its extent in the waveform's bar colour, `<ClipPlaceholderBody />` from ui-components); such a record has no clip in `tracks`, so look records up by `clipId`. `duration` counts loaded audio only. An export started while audio loads renders the loaded clips. `exportWav` takes `readonly` arrays for both of its arrays.

browser: `usePlaylistMixer().trackStates` has an entry for every track from the moment it enters the arrangement (no `undefined` entries); the values are the arrangement's mix. `useTrack()` no longer has `isLoading`: the track controls are enabled while a track's audio loads and a change made then is kept. `TrackContextValue` has a new required field, `isRecording`.

browser: `onReady` and the window event `waveform-playlist:ready` happen once per provider mount, the first time an engine is attached and no source of the arrangement is pending (a failed source counts as settled); they are not repeated after a later engine build. The event's `detail` is `{ trackCount, duration }` (every track; the loaded audio). After the first ready, `isReady` is false only while a later engine is built. Before the first ready `play()` does nothing and queues nothing (one console warning per mount in development); edits, the mixer, `seekTo` and the selection work.

browser: a track the arrangement refuses (wrong kind, a clip still loading on a MIDI track, a duplicate track or clip id) throws at the call that offered it: a render error from the `useCreateArrangement` initializer, an exception from `arrangement.addTrack()`. `onError` is called when the engine or its adapter cannot be built (only for a build that is still current) and when `onReady` throws; a failed source does not call it.

browser: `useClipDragHandlers` and `useClipSplitting` lose the options `tracks` and `engineRef` (and `samplesPerPixel`, #828: `useClipDragHandlers({ snapSamplePosition? })`, `useClipSplitting()`): they read the provider's arrangement and throw outside `WaveformPlaylistProvider`. A move, trim or split made while no engine is attached is kept and played by the next engine (it was dropped with a warning); a move or a trim commits the clip and track picked up at drag start, also when the row changed under the drag. The mixer setters, `reorderTrack`, `undo` and `redo` of `useEditorControls()` work with no engine attached, and the history survives an engine rebuild.

browser: while a take records, the track it records onto is shown muted: `trackStates` and its Mute button show it muted and its built-in `<TrackMuteButton />` is disabled (before, the mixer showed it unmuted). The track is marked as the take's (`arrangement.setTrackRecording`, #843), not muted in its mix: `arrangement.getMixer()` keeps its own mute, so a mute set during the take stays after it. An engine build that fails during playback now shows playback stopped.

recording: `useIntegratedRecording(arrangement, selectedTrackId, options?)` (was `(tracks, setTracks, selectedTrackId, options?)`). The hook reads the arrangement when a take starts and ends and adds the take, with the clips it trims, removes or splits, in one `arrangement.updateTrack()`: one undo step. `startRecording()` also refuses, through `error`, a selected id the arrangement does not hold (the take used to start and be lost at the end) and a track that holds a clip whose audio is still loading, failed to load or cannot be made from its audio. A take the arrangement refuses at the end sets `error` to `Recording completed but the take could not be saved on track "<id>": …`. The package now depends on `@waveform-playlist/engine` (for the `Arrangement` type).

ui-components: the draggable ids of a clip are keyed by identity: `clip/<trackId>/<clipId>` for the move, `clip-boundary-left/<trackId>/<clipId>` and `clip-boundary-right/<trackId>/<clipId>` for the trim handles (each part `encodeURIComponent`-encoded); they were `clip-<trackIndex>-<clipIndex>`, `clip-boundary-left-<trackIndex>-<clipIndex>` and `clip-boundary-right-<trackIndex>-<clipIndex>`, which is still what a `Clip` given no `trackId` uses. The drag data is unchanged. Code that matched the ids (a custom `onDragStart`, a collision detector, a test selector) matches the new form.

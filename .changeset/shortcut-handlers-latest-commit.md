'@waveform-playlist/browser': patch

Space and the Play button act on the last committed render. A press right after a render that did not come from an event, such as the one in which a selection finishes playing, used the render before: Space (`useKeyboardShortcuts`, and with it `<KeyboardShortcuts>`, `usePlaybackShortcuts` and `useAnnotationKeyboardControls`) ran the previous shortcuts and paused playback that had already ended instead of playing the selection again, and Space and the Play button (`usePlayFromSelectionOrCursor`) could play the previous selection, or ignore looping just turned on. A shortcut whose `enabled` option that render had just turned off could still fire once, and one just turned on could miss the press. Both hooks now take their copy of the shortcuts, `enabled`, the selection and the loop state when the render commits instead of after paint; `useKeyboardShortcuts` keeps one window listener for its whole lifetime and checks `enabled` on each key press. That listener is now added when the hook mounts, before paint, instead of after paint and again each time `enabled` turns on, so its place among other window `keydown` listeners can change: an app's listener added in the `useEffect` of a component rendered before the shortcuts, in the layout effect of a parent or of a component rendered after them, or while the shortcuts were disabled, used to run before the shortcut and now runs after it, and then sees `event.defaultPrevented` set for a shortcut that prevents the default (Space does). A listener in a parent's `useEffect` ran after the shortcut already, and capture-phase listeners still run first.

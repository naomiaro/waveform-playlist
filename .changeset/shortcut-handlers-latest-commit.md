Space and the Play button act on the last committed render. A press right after a render that did not come from an event, such as the one in which a selection finishes playing, used the render before: Space (`useKeyboardShortcuts`, and with it `<KeyboardShortcuts>`, `usePlaybackShortcuts` and `useAnnotationKeyboardControls`) ran the previous shortcuts and paused playback that had already ended instead of playing the selection again, and Space and the Play button (`usePlayFromSelectionOrCursor`) could play the previous selection, or ignore looping just turned on. A shortcut whose `enabled` option that render had just turned off could still fire once, and one just turned on could miss the press. Both hooks now take their copy of the shortcuts, `enabled`, the selection and the loop state when the render commits instead of after paint; `useKeyboardShortcuts` keeps one window listener for its whole lifetime and checks `enabled` on each key press.

`TempoMap` is now an immutable value. `setTempo`, `removeTempo` and `clearTempos` are replaced by `withTempo(bpm, atTick?, options?)`, `withoutTempo(atTick)` and `withoutTempos()`, each returning a new map and leaving the original untouched; `entries` exposes a frozen, tick-ordered snapshot with cumulative `secondsAtTick`. `Transport.setTempo/removeTempo/clearTempos` are unchanged in signature: the transport owns the current map (new `transport.tempoMap` getter) and swaps it into the scheduler, sample timeline, clip player and metronome on every edit (`Scheduler.setTempoMap`, `ClipPlayer.setTempoMap`, `MetronomePlayer.setTempoMap` added). `Scheduler.advance()` captures the map once per call, so a tempo edit made from inside a listener callback cannot change the window already being generated. `Scheduler.setTempoMap` re-anchors the scheduling cursor through seconds and the transport recomputes a bounded play's end tick, so a tempo change during playback neither regenerates nor skips already-scheduled time. `withTempo` validates `atTick`, copies and freezes curve interpolation options, and the entries constructor form is sealed behind a module-private token.

Precompiled Faust effect — native transport

tremolo.dsp was compiled ahead of time; the browser never runs the Faust compiler and @dawcore/faust is not loaded. The same effect is available two ways: as an AudioWorklet registered with registerEffect() (compiled with faust2wasm), and as a static WAM plugin (compiled with faust2wam). This page runs on NativePlayoutAdapter from @dawcore/transport.
